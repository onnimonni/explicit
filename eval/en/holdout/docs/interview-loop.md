# Engineering interview loop

How we interview backend engineers. Written for interviewers; candidates get a shorter public
version. The loop is four sessions over one day, remote or in Tampere.

## Sessions

| Session | Length | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Coding | 60 min | Small realistic task in the candidate's language |
| Design | 60 min | One system, pushed on trade-offs |
| Debugging | 45 min | A real past incident, anonymized |
| Values | 45 min | How they work with others |

Each session has has two interviewers: a lead who drives and a shadow who takes notes. The shadow
write the scorecard first so there view is not anchored by the lead.

## Coding

The task is a rate limiter or a log parser, chosen by the lead. We care about how the
candidate reads the problem, what they test, and weather they notice the edge
case we planted. Syntax slips are irrelevant, they have an editor at work too.

Let them use they're own tools. If they want an AI assistant, allow it and
ask them to narrate what they except and reject from it; that tells you more
then watching them type.

## Design

Ask for something the candidate has built a version of before. Not a URL shortener. Push on one axis at a time: what breaks at 10x,
what happens when the primary dies, how would you prioritise the first
week of work. A strong candidate dissagrees with you at least once and explains why.

## Debugging

Show the alert, the dashboard and the logs from a past incident, redacted
where needed. Reveal information as they ask for it. The goal is not the answer; its
the questions. Candidates who jump to a fix without reading the timeline get a gentle nudge,
once.

## Values

No trick questions. Ask for specific stories: a disagreement they lost, project
they killed, feedback that changed how they work. You're job is to listen for
the difference between "we" that means teamwork and "we" that means someone else did it.

## Scoring

Four levels: strong no, no, yes, strong yes, with a written reason. "Culture fit" is not a
reason. The debrief happens within 48 hours; the hiring manager decides, the panel advises.
If the panel splits two and two, we hire nobody and fix the loop, not the candidate.

## Logistics

Interviewers from Helsinki, Tampere and Stockholm rotate. Sofia Lindgren
owns the schedule; Ilkka Saarinen owns the question bank and retiers any
question that leaks online.
