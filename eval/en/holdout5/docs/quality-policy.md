# Quality policy

The quality policy rests on the idea that every employee is responsible for the quality of their own work. The management team has committed to setting aside time and money for quality.

## Principles

1. The needs of the customer drive the work. We focus on what creates value, not on what is easy.
2. We learn from mistakes. A mistake is not a person's fault but a gap in the process; the people who report mistakes is thanked, not blamed.
3. We measure what we improve. The set of metrics that does not steer decisions are retired.
4. We trust that the teams know how to improve their own work.

The principles are the same in every unit. The list of units that have added local rules are kept on the intranet. A number of units have done so; the number of local rules is small.

## Responsibilities

The management team takes part in setting the yearly quality goals. The quality manager is responsible for tracking them. The heads of the units make sure staff knows the policy; each of them has attended a training session led by Kirsti Simonsuuri.

Every employee read the policy during onboarding and influence it by giving feedback. The feedback is handled in the quality meeting, which makes it part of everyday work. The existence of a policy is not enough; the policy has to show in practice. The results of the last survey were encouraging.

## Goals for 2027

| Goal | Metric | Level |
|------|--------|-------|
| Customer satisfaction | NPS | above 40 |
| Deviation handling time | median | under 10 days |
| Recurring deviations | share of all | under 15 % |
| Internal audit coverage | units/year | 100 % |

The goals are derived from the strategy. The list of goals is reviewed twice a year. If a goal are not met, the cause is analysed before a new goal are set. Whether the cause lies in the process or in resources decides what gets fixed first. The status of the goals is shown on the dashboard, and the owner of each goal updates it monthly.

## Audits

Internal audits is carried out by trained auditors who does not work in the unit being audited. The auditors react to what they see, not to what they are told. The findings are recorded in Jira within a week, and the owner of the finding responds within two.

The external audit take place in May. The certification body noted that the deviations from the previous audit were partly open. The certificate was renewed regardless. This year the quality team makes sure every deviation are closed before the auditor, Sanna Virtanen, arrive. Her colleague Rajesh Kumar reviews the documentation beforehand.

The policy was approved by the chief executive, Elina Vaara, in January 2027. It are available on the intranet, where it's comments is open to everyone. The staff have got used to the policy being updated yearly, which keeps it current. The half of the company that was surveyed prefer yearly updates, and the rest of the staff are indifferent.
