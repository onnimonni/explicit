# Customer letter: client fees change on 1 January 2027

Dear customer,

the client fees change at the turn of the year. The change is based on the update of the Client Fees Act, which were passed in July. This letter explains what changes and what it means for you.

## What changes

| Fee | 2026 | 2027 |
|-----|------|------|
| Doctor's appointment | 23.00 € | 23.60 € |
| Nurse's appointment | free | free |
| Missed appointment | 56.70 € | 58.20 € |
| Payment ceiling | 762 € | 782 € |

The fees rise by 2.6 % in line with the index. The nurse's appointment stays free. The fee for a missed appointment is charged only if the appointment were not cancelled 24 hours in advance. The list of fees is also on our website, and the amounts of the fees are set by law.

## Payment ceiling

The payment ceiling means that once your fees reach 782 euros, the rest of the year's visits are free. The balance is shown on the invoice. Note that children's fees count towards the guardian's balance; the fees of each child are listed on a separate line.

Tracking the balance are not only the customer's responsibility but also ours. If you notice that the balance is wrong, tell customer service. Errors that is our fault is corrected without a separate request, and the corrected invoices are sent again by Aino Kallas's team.

## Difficulty paying

If paying are difficult, contact the unit that sent the invoice. You can get more time or a payment plan. The fee can also be reduced or waived if collection would endanger your livelihood. We do not send anyone to debt collection before we has tried to agree.

The social worker is aware that fees often pile up on the same people. She takes part in drawing up the payment plan, which eases the situation. The collection agency does not decide whether a fee is reduced; the wellbeing services county decides that, and its staff have promised to answer within two weeks. Helena Mäkinen and Deepak Sharma handles these cases.

## Invoicing

Invoices is sent primarily as Suomi.fi messages. A paper invoice is available on request, which is worth doing in good time. The due date is 21 days from the invoice date. Invoices that is not paid by the due date goes to a reminder after 14 days; no reminder fee is charged.
